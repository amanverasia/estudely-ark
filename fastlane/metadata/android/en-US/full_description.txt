Estudely Ark puts a library on your phone that keeps working with no internet: health books, an encyclopedia, a map of all of India, repair guides, school science and programming manuals.

Pick the parts you want and the app downloads them once. After that, everything works offline.

<b>What's in the library</b>
* General knowledge: Simple English Wikipedia with pictures, CIA World Factbook
* Health and sanitation: all 16 Hesperian health books, Wikipedia Medicine, water and sanitation, nursing textbooks
* Food and growing: food preparation and storage, gardening
* Repair and practical skills: Appropedia, repair wiki, knots
* Map of India: every street, with offline search for towns, villages, hospitals and stations
* Education: science simulations, school textbooks, maths, physics and chemistry
* Computing: programming manuals, freeCodeCamp, Arch Wiki
* Optional: iFixit repair guides, Energypedia

<b>How it works</b>
* Downloads use BitTorrent from other people who have the library, plus an always-on server. Downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data.
* Every part is checked against a signed list of files before it opens, so you know nothing was changed or damaged on the way.
* Search inside each book and the map, with no internet.
* Keep the library on phone storage or an SD card, and read in light or dark mode with your own text size.
* Optional sharing: on Wi-Fi while charging, your phone can pass the library on to others. It is off until you switch it on.

<b>Free, no ads, no accounts</b>
The app and the library are free and stay free. There are no ads, no tracking and no sign-in. The library is the same as the Estudely Ark desktop pack (ark.esdy.cc).

The content comes from Wikipedia, Hesperian, Kiwix, OpenStreetMap, Appropedia, iFixit, PhET and others, under their own licences. Some of it (Hesperian, PhET, iFixit) may only be shared for free.
