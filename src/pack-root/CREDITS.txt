ESTUDELY ARK: CREDITS AND LICENCES
==================================

This pack collects free material made by others. All credit belongs to them. The pack is
given away free and is not sold. If you can afford to, please support these projects.

Archives in .zim format were packaged by the Kiwix project and openZIM
(https://kiwix.org). Unless noted, the text of each archive keeps the licence of its source.

NON-COMMERCIAL ITEMS. Several parts of this pack may only be shared non-commercially:
the Hesperian books, the PhET simulations, the iFixit guides and most OpenStax textbooks. Giving the pack away,
copying it for friends, schools and clinics, or charging only the cost of a USB drive is
fine. Selling it for profit is not.

Licences below were checked against each source on 16 November 2026.
