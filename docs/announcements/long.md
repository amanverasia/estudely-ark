**Estudely Ark: a free offline library for India, now with an Android app (Wikipedia, health books, full India map, textbooks)**

I've put together a collection of useful knowledge that you download once and keep. It works with no internet:

- Simple English Wikipedia with pictures, plus Wikipedia Medicine
- All 16 English Hesperian health books (including *Where There Is No Doctor*), nursing textbooks, water and sanitation guides
- A map of all of India down to every street, with offline search for towns, villages, hospitals and stations
- PhET science simulations, LibreTexts K-12, OpenStax textbooks
- Repair guides, food and gardening, programming manuals
- Optional: iFixit repair guides, and (on computers) Gemma 4 AI running on your own machine

**On a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux):** get it by torrent and choose only the folders you want (core is 13.5 GB; everything is 32 GB). Double-click Start-Library and it opens in your browser. One computer can share it with a whole room over Wi-Fi, and you can copy it to others by USB.

**On an Android phone:** the Estudely Ark app (Android 8 or newer) lets you pick the subjects you want, downloads them over BitTorrent (Wi-Fi only unless you allow mobile data), and opens them offline: books and manuals with search, PDFs (with pinch zoom, and it remembers your page), and the India map with place search. You can keep the library on an SD card, and if you like, the app can share it with others while your phone is on Wi-Fi and charging. Download it from GitHub; an F-Droid listing is under review.

Every copy is signed: on a computer you can check it with the Verify tool, and the app checks every file before it opens. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app is open source (GPL-3.0).

It's free and non-commercial; please don't sell copies. Please seed if you can.
