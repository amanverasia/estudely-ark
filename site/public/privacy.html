Privacy

Estudely Ark Android app (cc.esdy.ark) and the offline library · last updated 17 November 2026

Short version

The app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive any data from your phone.

What stays on your phone

The parts of the library you download, your choice of parts, your settings (Wi-Fi only, sharing on or off) and a count of how much you have shared. They are stored in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What other computers can see

The library is downloaded with BitTorrent. Like any BitTorrent app, while it downloads or shares:

  • other people downloading or sharing the same library, and the public BitTorrent trackers and DHT network that help them find each other, can see your phone's IP address and which parts of the library it is downloading or sharing;
  • if no one shares quickly enough, the app downloads files directly from the Internet Archive (archive.org), which sees your IP address as any website does.

The app sends nothing else: no name, no phone number, no contacts, no location and no device identifiers.

Sharing

Sharing is off until you switch it on. When it is on, the app shares the parts you have downloaded with other people, only on Wi-Fi and only while the phone is charging.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download the library and to check for Wi-Fi.
  • Foreground service and notifications: to keep a download running and show its progress.
  • Run at start-up: so sharing (if you switched it on) continues after a restart.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/amanverasia/estudely-ark/issues.
